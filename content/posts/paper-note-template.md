## TL;DR (3 sentences max)
- What problem does the paper tackle?
- Why does their approach matter?
- What is the single biggest takeaway for you?

## Bibliographic Snapshot
| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Citation | `Author et al., Venue Year` |
| Keywords | learning, systems, efficiency |
| Dataset / Benchmarks | e.g., ImageNet, GLUE |
| Code / Repo | https://github.com/org/project |

## Problem Statement
Summarize the research question and constraints. Mention assumptions, threat model, or motivating application in 3-4 sentences.

## Core Idea
Document the proposed method or architecture. Use bullets or short paragraphs to note:
1. Main components or modules
2. Training regimen / algorithm steps
3. Any equations or loss terms you want to revisit (embed snippets if needed)

## Visual / Diagram Notes
Describe figures that clarified the idea. Capture axis labels, legends, or surprising trends so you can redraw them later.

## Key Results
- Metric + baseline + improvement (e.g., "Top-1 +2.3% vs. ResNet50")
- Ablations or sensitivity studies worth remembering
- Limitations the authors acknowledged

## Personal Analysis
**What worked**: Observations you agree with or want to replicate.

**What puzzled you**: Gaps, unclear derivations, or experimental weaknesses.

## Connections & Related Work
Link this paper to others you've read. Note whether it extends, contradicts, or replaces previous approaches.

## Implementation Sketch
List concrete steps if you were to reproduce the work:
- Dependencies or frameworks
- Data preprocessing pipeline
- Training recipe, hyperparameters, and compute budget

## Open Questions / Next Actions
- Experiments you want to try next
- Follow-up papers or citations to chase
- Ideas for blog posts, talks, or demos derived from this paper

## Glossary (optional)
Define any new terms or symbols so future-you can skim quickly.
